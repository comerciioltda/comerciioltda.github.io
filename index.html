    FESTILUME™ QUESTIONS

    Everything You Need to Know

    Quick answers about setup, designs, controls and your Festilume™ lights.

    ✨ How Does Festilume™ Work? +
    Hang the LED curtain across your balcony or railing, connect it, then choose your favorite design. In minutes, your balcony becomes an animated Christmas display.
    ⚡ Is It Easy to Set Up? +
    Yes! Festilume™ is designed for a simple setup. Secure the top section, let the LED strands hang down, connect the controller and you're ready to light it up.
    🎅 What Designs Can It Display? +
    Choose from loads of colorful animated designs including Santa, snowmen, reindeer, Christmas trees, snowflakes and more.
    📱 Can I Control It From My Phone? +
    Yes. Use your phone to switch designs, change effects and customize your Festilume™ display without taking the lights down.
    🌨️ Can I Use Festilume™ Outdoors? +
    Festilume™ is made for festive indoor and outdoor decorating. Always follow the included installation and weather-use instructions when setting it up outside.
    📦 When Will My Order Arrive? +
    Once your order ships, you'll receive tracking so you can follow your Festilume™ order all the way home.
